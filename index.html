Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

PostFlow Labs operates PostFlow Automation, a workflow designed to assist authorized users with preparing and publishing content to their LinkedIn accounts.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the application may access limited LinkedIn account information necessary for authentication and content publishing.

This may include:

  • Basic LinkedIn profile information
  • LinkedIn member identifiers
  • Authorization information required to access LinkedIn APIs
  • Content and media submitted by the user for publishing

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the application's functionality, including:

  • Authenticating authorized users
  • Preparing LinkedIn posts
  • Uploading user-provided or generated media
  • Publishing content to LinkedIn after authorization
  • Maintaining workflow history and troubleshooting errors

3. Data Storage

Application metadata may be stored securely using services such as Supabase for workflow history, analytics, and operational purposes. Uploaded media may also be stored in private storage when required for the workflow.

Authentication credentials and API secrets are intended to remain within secure server-side systems and are not intentionally exposed to public users.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services required to provide its functionality, including LinkedIn, n8n, Supabase, and other content processing or media services.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Sharing

PostFlow Labs does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers when necessary to operate the requested automation or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, or analyze the service.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of stored information associated with their use of PostFlow Automation by contacting the application owner.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect stored information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application evolves. The latest version will be published on this page.
